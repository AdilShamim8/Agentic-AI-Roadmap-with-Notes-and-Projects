# 10 - Interview preparation

The curriculum teaches you what to build. This module teaches you how to get selected: the rounds, the rubrics, the question banks, the mock interviews, and the four-week sprint that turns the other nine modules into offers.

It goes deeper than the [field guide's interview chapter](../field-guide/05-interview-prep.md): full design cases with whiteboard transcripts, a behavioral bank with STAR skeletons, three complete mock interviews with examiner commentary, and per-module question banks under [interview/questions/](../interview/questions/).

## What you will learn

- What each round screens for and how hiring decisions actually get made
- The six-part structure for agent system design rounds, applied to five worked cases
- How to run the coding screen and the trace-reading round (trajectory debugging)
- How to scope, build, and present a take-home that survives the walkthrough
- A 30-question behavioral bank in STAR format, mapped to the projects in this repo
- Resume, portfolio, and GitHub hygiene for agentic AI roles
- Negotiation against the published salary bands, and how to decide between offers
