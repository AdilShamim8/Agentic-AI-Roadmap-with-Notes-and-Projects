# Question banks

Technical interview questions per curriculum module, with model answers. Two files per module: concepts (the definitions and mechanisms screeners probe) and scenarios (the applied questions onsite rounds use). Every question names the chapter that teaches it.

Use them two ways: cover the answer column and self-grade before a loop, and lift the question set to drill with a partner - one asks, one answers in under two minutes, the [interviewer rubric](../../10-interview-preparation/26-interviewer-rubric.md) as the shared standard.

## The answer convention

Model answers are calibrated to two minutes spoken. Read yours against three bars: does it name the mechanism, not just the vocabulary; does it survive one follow-up ("why?"); and does it end without hedging. An answer that cannot clear those three is the question to restudy - the chapter link is in each question's last line.
